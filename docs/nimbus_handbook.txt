NIMBUS LABS - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (sample document, the company is fictional)

1. Office hours
Our office in Salt Lake Sector V, Kolkata is open from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Core hours, when everyone must be available for meetings, are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Saturdays and Sundays are holidays.

2. Work from home
Employees may work from home on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Working from home on any other day needs written approval from your team lead at least one day in advance. New employees can work from home only after completing their first 3 months.

3. Leave policy
Every full-time employee gets 24 paid leave days per year. Up to 8 unused leave days can be carried forward to the next year; the rest expire on 31 March. Sick leave is separate: 10 days per year, and a doctor's note is needed for more than 2 sick days in a row. Leave requests must be sent through the HR portal at least 7 days before the leave starts, except for sick leave.

4. Laptop and equipment
Each employee gets a company laptop. You can request a new laptop every 3 years, with a budget of up to Rs 60,000. Lost or damaged laptops must be reported to the IT desk within 24 hours. Do not install pirated software on company laptops.

5. Health insurance
All employees are covered by health insurance of Rs 5,00,000 per year. Spouse and up to two children can be added for free. Parents can be added for an extra Rs 4,000 per year, paid by the employee.

6. Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of Rs 25,000 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry forward. To use it, get approval from your team lead and submit the receipt to HR within 30 days.

7. Notice period
The notice period is 60 days for employees after confirmation, and 15 days during the first 3 months (probation period).
